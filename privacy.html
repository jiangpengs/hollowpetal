HollowPetal Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

HollowPetal (hereinafter referred to as "this App") respects and protects the privacy of all users. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use this App. This App is a standalone card game. No account registration is required, and we do not actively collect or upload your personally identifiable information.

1. What Information We Collect

This App does not actively collect your name, phone number, email address, location, photo library, contacts or other sensitive personal information.

  • Local game save data: Game progress, in-game settings and preference configurations are stored only locally on your device and will not be automatically uploaded to our servers. Save data will be deleted together with the App upon uninstallation.
  • Crash logs (optional): If you enable system crash reporting, the logs are only used to diagnose program bugs and do not contain information that can identify you.

We do not collect any user-generated content. This App has no functions for posting, commenting, chatting or social feeds.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Local save data is only used to restore your game progress on your device;
  • Anonymous crash information is only used to fix bugs and improve game stability;
  • We will not sell, share or rent any information to third parties, nor use it for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

This App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising or login SDKs. No third party collects your information through this App.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

All game saves are stored locally on your device. You may remove your data at any time by:

  • Resetting game progress within the App;
  • Uninstalling this App to permanently erase all local game data.

We do not store any of your game data on cloud servers and cannot remotely access or delete save files on your device.

5. Children’s Privacy

This App is intended for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for deletion.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not hold any of your personal information, you may clear all local game data at any time by uninstalling the App.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy during app updates. The revised version will be posted on this page. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
